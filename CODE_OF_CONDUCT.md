# Code of Conduct

## Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Be respectful, accept constructive criticism, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Contact maintainers by opening a GitHub issue: https://github.com/JayCommit/lean-ui/issues

Maintainers will review reports and may remove, edit, or reject comments, code, and other contributions, or ban a contributor. This summary does not publish a private email address.
